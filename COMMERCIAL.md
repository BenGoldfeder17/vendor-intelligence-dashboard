This project is released under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). **Commercial use is not granted
by that licence.**

## Free — no permission needed

- Personal projects, study, experimentation, hobby use
- Academic and educational institutions
- Charities, nonprofits, public research bodies
- Government institutions
- Evaluating the software before deciding whether to license it

## Requires a commercial licence

- Any use inside a for-profit company, including internal tooling
- Bundling it into a product or service you sell
- Running it as a hosted or managed service
- Consulting or agency work delivered to clients

If you are unsure which side you fall on, ask — the answer is usually quick.

## Getting a licence

Open an issue titled **"Commercial licence enquiry"**, or reach the copyright
holder via the GitHub profile linked in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Useful details:

- Your organisation and what it does
- How you intend to use the software
- Rough scale (internal team, customer-facing, number of users)

Terms are negotiable. The intent of this licence is not to block use — it is to
keep the option of a conversation before someone builds a business on it.
